The data

Quote a load without guessing at the fuel.

A cross-border quote needs two numbers that agree: what diesel costs where you load, and what it costs where you deliver. Most carriers price one side and hope. This gives you both, converted to the same basis, on the day the source published it.

  • Cheapest fuel right now is Alberta at 231.2¢/L. If your return leg runs through it, that is where to fill.
  • The Quebec figure is 290.2¢/L, 59.0¢ above it. That gap is what a single tank can move.
  • Longest booth wait reporting is Peace Bridge at 13 min. Build the appointment around that one, not the average.
  • Free, no account, no paywall. Every figure carries the date the source published it.

The writing

Why the two numbers are not comparable as published.

Canada quotes diesel by the litre, the US by the gallon, and neither is a price you can compare until you convert both. That is the easy one. The harder ones are what the CRA asks before it calls your corporation a personal services business, and which IFTA filing mistakes trigger an audit. Written for the person running the truck.
